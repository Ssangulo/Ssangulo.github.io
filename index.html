Molecular ecology · Mycology · Metabarcoding

I study the fungi that live alongside animals and plants.

My work uses DNA metabarcoding and field sampling to ask how fungal communities are shaped by their hosts, their seasons and their landscapes — currently in Norwegian alpine tundra, previously along Andean elevational gradients.

About

I am a biologist working at the interface of mycology, community ecology and molecular methods. Most of my research runs on high-throughput amplicon sequencing — ITS metabarcoding of fungi, plant ITS2 for diet reconstruction — combined with the fieldwork needed to collect samples that are actually worth sequencing.

I build my own bioinformatic pipelines end to end: from demultiplexing and primer trimming through DADA2 denoising, OTU curation, taxonomic assignment against UNITE, and on to the community models that answer the ecological question — ordination and PERMANOVA, Hill-number diversity, joint species distribution models and Bayesian hierarchical regression.

Everything I publish comes with the code that produced it.
